# JEMIX Academy - locked rules

These rules are mandatory for all JEMIX Academy SCORM lessons.

## Core decision

Build lessons one by one. Do not redesign the platform or architecture while producing Module 1.

## Visual identity

- JEMIX has no blue in the 2025 visual identity.
- Use only white, graphite/black, light gray and JEMIX red accents.
- The left table of contents/sidebar must have a white background.
- Navigation/action buttons may stay red/current accent color.
- The original JEMIX logo must be used exactly as supplied by the user. Do not redraw, approximate, or generate it.
- Product images must be real product images, preferably from terrawater.ru or supplied assets. Do not use AI-generated pumps for final lessons.

## Lesson UI requirements

- Overall lesson screen should be about 15 percent more compact than the first alpha version.
- The source-pump-house connection must be readable and understandable, not decorative.
- Do not show technical/internal fields on learner screens: type, ux_title, Goal, layout, id, screen, etc.
- There must be a clear visible button/control for audio: "Прослушать".
- Use Russian learner-facing labels, not English metadata.

## Lesson workflow

- Work by lessons, not by files.
- Finish and approve lesson 1.1 before using its template for the rest of Module 1.
- After lesson 1.1 is approved, build lesson 1.2, then 1.3, etc.
- Do not re-open global architecture discussions unless the user explicitly asks.

## Current approved direction for lesson 1.1

- Original JEMIX logo in the upper-left/brand area.
- White sidebar/table of contents.
- Red buttons remain.
- Real product photo area.
- Clear goals screen: "После урока вы сможете".
- Lesson explanation screen with visible audio control.
- Clear scheme: "Источник - Насос - Дом" with explanations.
- Mini-practice/test at the end.
